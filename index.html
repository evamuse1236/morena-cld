These numbers show that women and men in Morena have very different outcomes. On their own, they don't show why. That is what the loops try to explain.

What we don't know yet
  • How much say women in Morena have today. No district survey asks who decides about work, money and marriage.
  • Whether the early marriage loop still works the same way. The main study is from 2009.
  • Whether giving women more say lowers early marriage for their daughters. The map suggests it would, but nobody has tested it.
  • How common violence against women is in Morena. There is a state figure (28.1% of married women in Madhya Pradesh reported violence by their husband, NFHS-53), but no reliable figure for the district alone.
  • Whether the schemes work in Morena. No study has measured how much Ladli Laxmi or Beti Bachao Beti Padhao lowered early marriage in the district.
  • Whether any scheme helps Bedia households find other work or face less stigma.
  • Other reasons women don't do paid work, such as few local jobs, housework and childcare, or unsafe travel. The map doesn't separate these out.

Good questions to ask people in Morena:

  • When a woman starts earning, who decides how the money is spent?
  • When a girl leaves school, what happens first: a marriage proposal, a safety worry, money trouble, or no school nearby?
  • Can mothers who are against early marriage stop it for their daughters?
